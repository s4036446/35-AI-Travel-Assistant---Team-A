# Initial Requirements: Staff QR Scan Flow

**Project:** AI Travel Assistant (Your Accessible Flight)
**Prepared by:** Wen Bin Liang (BA)
**Task:** [Design & Bootstrap] - Draft initial requirements from client discussion

## 1. Problem Statement

Travelers with disabilities currently face significant uncertainty and delay when navigating airports, because critical information such as wheelchair dimensions, aircraft door dimensions, and mobility aid battery capacity which isn't readily accessible to airport staff at the point of need. As a result, passengers are forced to arrive up to 5 hours before their flight "just to be safe," when the actual assistance process typically only takes 40 minutes to an hour, largely because wheelchairs are classified as dangerous goods requiring careful handling. Your Accessible Flight aims to close this information gap so that safe, efficient assistance can happen in as little as 20 minutes, rather than hours.

## 2. Target Users

- **Primary:** Airport ground staff and gate agents
  - They need instant, accurate access to a passenger's wheelchair specifications (dimensions, battery capacity) to make safe handling decisions quickly.
- **Secondary:** Travelers with a disability (or their caregiver)
  - They benefit from a shorter, less stressful airport experience.

## 3. What Already Exists

A passenger-facing app already exists and is functional. It currently allows a passenger to:

- Select their airline and flight route
- Enter their mobility equipment details (type, dimensions, weight, battery type, spare batteries)
- Get instant pass/fail verification against that airline's specific requirements (e.g. cargo hold dimensions, IATA battery provisions)
- Generate a QR code encoding the verified trip + equipment result
- View a history of past saved verifications

This app is not being rebuilt. It is the input side of the system this project's feature depends on.

## 4. Candidate Main Feature: Staff QR Scan

This is the feature Sprint 2 will build, the counterpart to the existing passenger app.

**Core flow:**

- Staff member opens the staff app and scans a passenger's QR code (generated by the existing passenger app)
- App instantly retrieves and displays the passenger's verified mobility equipment information
- Staff can see at a glance whether the equipment is compliant, and the specific dimensions/battery/spare battery details relevant to loading and handling

**Data expected to be shown on the results screen:**

- Airline and destination/route
- Equipment type (e.g. Wheelchair Electric)
- Dimensions and weight
- Battery type, quantity, and removability
- Spare battery count
- Overall pass/fail status per category, matching the categories already verified passenger-side (Equipment Type, Dimensions and Weight, Battery Type, Spare Batteries)

**Assumed functionality (need confirmation, flagged for follow-up, see Section 6):**

- Staff app runs on Android (assumed)
- Scanning is done via the device's built-in camera, no dedicated scanner hardware required (assumed)
- Staff app requires its own login/authentication, separate from passenger accounts (assumed)
- The QR code is a reference/ID that the staff app looks up live in Firestore, rather than the full data being encoded directly in the QR image itself (assumed)

## 5. Scope Boundaries

- **In scope this term:** The staff QR scan flow described above, plus the underlying Firebase/Firestore infrastructure needed to support it.
- **Out of scope this term:** The accessible trip-planning "story" search feature (hotels, museums, sightseeing, excursions, rental, repair). This was raised by the client as a future direction but explicitly excluded from this term's delivery in the agreed proposal.

## 6. Questions (Needs Client/Team Confirmation)

- In the client notes, it is assumed that the staff app we are building is on "Android," but the passenger app was directly downloaded and used on iOS, can we confirm whether the staff app is cross-platform or only on Android?
- Does the staff app require any form of login before use? If so, is it a shared/generic team login, individual staff accounts (for accountability/audit purposes), or role-based accounts with different permission levels?
- Does the QR code encode the full result data directly, or just a reference ID looked up from Firestore at scan time? (This affects whether the staff app needs to work offline.)
- Does the staff app need to log/store scan history (e.g. for audit purposes, tracking which staff member handled which passenger)? Not currently mentioned anywhere.
- Should the staff app also perform a door-compatibility check, given this was mentioned in the original client notes but isn't visible in the current passenger app's verification flow?
- Should staff be able to manually flag or override a result (e.g. if they physically inspect equipment that doesn't match the recorded specs)?
- Is offline/low-connectivity handling required for the staff app, given airport network conditions can be unreliable?
